    W_{l} = \begin{bmatrix}
    a & b
    \end{bmatrix}, W_{l+1} \begin{bmatrix}
    c\\
    d
    \end{bmatrix}
\]

\[
    W_{l_{1}} W_{l_{0}} = \begin{bmatrix}
    ac & bc\\
    ad & bd
    \end{bmatrix}
\]
Summing over these entries gives $ac+bc+ad+bd= (c+d)(a+b)$
And through the routing matrix:
\[
    (a+b) * (c+d)
